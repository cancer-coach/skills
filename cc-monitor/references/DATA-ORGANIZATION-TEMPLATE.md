# Practical Records and Follow-Through Templates

Sources checked: **2026-10-15**. These are optional drafts for the person's review, not a medical record system or a care plan generated by AI. Start with one useful page; exhaustive record collection is not required.

## Privacy before paperwork

Use an alias and the minimum information needed. Do not put health records in a public repository, issue, shared link, or deployment. Ask before saving sensitive material, recording a visit, enabling cloud sync, or sending anything. Verify the recipient and approved secure channel. Review the AI service's privacy, retention and training settings; do not assume clinical confidentiality or HIPAA coverage.

Genomic data can remain identifying after names are removed and can reveal information about relatives. Public release can be difficult to reverse. [NHGRI: Privacy in Genomics](https://www.genome.gov/about-genomics/policy-issues/Privacy).

## Medication reconciliation for clinician/pharmacist review

Copy the current instructions; never invent or change a dose, schedule, start, stop, or hold instruction. Include prescription medicines, nonprescription products, vitamins, herbs, supplements, allergies, and reactions. Flag discrepancies for the prescriber instead of resolving them yourself.

```markdown
| Product | Instructions as recorded | What I am actually taking | Prescriber/source/date | Question or discrepancy |
|---------|--------------------------|---------------------------|------------------------|-------------------------|
| | | | | |
Allergies / reactions to verify:
Reviewed with clinician or pharmacist / date:
```

## Visit summary: draft until verified

```markdown
Visit date / clinician:
Patient's recollection (label separately from the official note):
Clinician's explanation, as documented:
Decisions and alternatives discussed:
Questions still unanswered:
| Action | Owner | Due date supplied by team | How completion will be confirmed |
|--------|-------|---------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| | | | |
Medication instructions needing confirmation:
Test result expected / who reviews / contact if overdue:
Next appointment and purpose:
After-hours contact and team-specific urgent instructions:
Verified against official note or with team / date:
Corrections requested / unresolved discrepancy:
```

Do not present inferred diagnoses, promises, or an AI summary as the clinician's statement. Request corrections through the team's usual channel; do not send the draft automatically.

## Symptom and recovery notes

```markdown
| Date/time | Symptom or functional change | Effect on eating, drinking, sleep or activity | Team contacted / instructions received |
|-----------|------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| | | | |
Recovery question / rehab, nutrition, psychosocial or work support needed:
Activity or wound restrictions to confirm with the team:
```

Ask the team for a written follow-up care plan, including who coordinates it and whom to call about new symptoms. [NCI: Follow-Up Medical Care](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/coping/survivorship/follow-up-care).

During chemotherapy, **38°C / 100.4°F or higher requires immediate oncology contact and urgent assessment**; do not wait to fill in a diary. [CDC: Watch Out for Fever](https://www.cdc.gov/cancer-preventing-infections/patients/fever.html). Severe or rapidly worsening symptoms require emergency care using local services. These examples are not exhaustive; follow the team's written action plan.
