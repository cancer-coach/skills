# MRD and Biomarker Tracking Reference

| Platform | Company | Approach | Cancer Types | Sensitivity | Cost/Test |
|----------|---------|---------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| Signatera | Natera | Tumor-informed (16 custom variants) | CRC, breast, bladder, lung, ovarian | ~0.01% VAF | $3,000-$5,000 |

## Tumor Markers by Cancer Type

| Cancer | Primary Markers | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| Colorectal | CEA | Most validated for monitoring |
| Breast | CA 15-3, CA 27-29 | Not all breast cancers produce these |
| Ovarian | CA-125, HE4 | CA-125 fluctuates — track trends |
| Prostate | PSA | Very sensitive early recurrence indicator |
| Pancreatic | CA 19-9 | Also elevated in benign conditions |
| Liver/HCC | AFP | Also elevated in germ cell tumors |
| Testicular | AFP, beta-hCG, LDH | Critical for staging and monitoring |
| Thyroid | Thyroglobulin | After thyroidectomy |
| Lung | None reliably validated | ctDNA increasingly important |

## Immune and Organ Function Markers

| Category | Markers | What to Watch |
|----------|---------|--------------|
| Immune | ALC, NLR | Low ALC may impair immunotherapy |
| Inflammation | CRP, ESR | Rising may indicate disease activity |
| Liver | AST, ALT, bilirubin, albumin | Treatment toxicity |
| Kidney | Creatinine, BUN, GFR | Cisplatin, contrast dye |
| Cardiac | Troponin, BNP | HER2 agents, anthracyclines |
| Thyroid | TSH, free T4 | Immunotherapy thyroiditis |
| Nutritional | Albumin, prealbumin, weight | Prognostic factor |
