# Understanding a Response Assessment

Sources checked: **2026-10-15**. Translate documented terms and prepare questions. The radiologist, pathologist and treating team assess response; an AI assistant must not independently classify scans or recommend treatment continuation or discontinuation.

## Frameworks you may encounter

| Framework | What to clarify |
|-----------|-----------------|
| RECIST | How selected solid-tumor lesions and other findings were assessed |
| iRECIST | Whether a trial's immune-response reporting rules apply |
| Metabolic imaging criteria | Which method and acquisition conditions were used |
| Pathological response | What the surgical specimen showed, using the cancer-specific definition |
| Blood-cancer criteria | Which disease-specific system applies |

Measurement rules are technical and context dependent. A percentage copied from one lesion or report is not enough to establish response or progression.

iRECIST confirmation rules support trial reporting. Possible pseudoprogression does not establish that growth is harmless. Continued treatment beyond initial progression is a clinical decision that takes stability and symptoms into account. [RECIST Working Group: iRECIST](https://recist.eortc.org/irecist/).

## Report discussion template

```markdown
Report date and source:
Comparison study and date, as reported:
Response wording copied from report:
New or uncertain findings to ask about:
Symptoms and functional changes to tell the team:
Team's interpretation and explanation:
Agreed next step / clinician / date:
```

Ask: “How do the scan, examination, symptoms and laboratory results fit together? What is uncertain? What should I report before the next planned assessment?” Do not delay assessment of worsening symptoms while waiting for a confirmatory scan.
