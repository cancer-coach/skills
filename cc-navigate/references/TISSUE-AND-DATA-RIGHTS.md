# Tissue and Data Rights

## Your Legal Rights

### HIPAA
Right to access your complete medical record within 30 days. Providers cannot deny access. May charge reasonable copying fees. Electronic records in electronic format if requested.

### 21st Century Cures Act
Strengthened access to electronic health information. Prohibits "information blocking." Test results, clinical notes, and data must be available without unnecessary delay.

### Tissue Rights
Laws vary by state but generally: you can request tissue samples (FFPE blocks, slides) be sent to another institution. Hospital may retain a portion.

## Template: Tissue Request Letter

```
[Date]
[Hospital Pathology Department]

Re: Tissue Sample Request — [Patient Name, DOB, MRN]

I request release of tissue samples from my [procedure]
performed on [date].

I request:
- [ ] FFPE tissue blocks
- [ ] Unstained slides
- [ ] H&E stained slides
- [ ] Fresh-frozen tissue (if available)

Please send to: [Receiving lab name and address]

I understand the hospital may retain portions for records.

[Patient Name, Contact Info]
```

## Template: Medical Records Request

```
[Date]
[Hospital Medical Records]

Re: Complete Records — [Patient Name, DOB, MRN]

Pursuant to HIPAA, I request complete copies of:
- All clinical and progress notes
- All imaging reports and DICOM files
- All laboratory results
- All pathology reports
- All genomic/molecular reports
- All operative reports
- All consultation notes

Electronic format where available. Within 30 days per law.

[Patient Name, Contact Info]
```

## Genomic Data Ownership
- You own your genomic data
- Request raw files (FASTQ, BAM, VCF), not just summary reports
- Store securely with encrypted cloud storage
- Data can be reanalyzed as knowledge advances

## Open Data Sharing
Sid made 25TB public at osteosarc.com — WGS, WES, RNA-seq, scRNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, imaging. Even partial, anonymized sharing advances science and helps future patients.
