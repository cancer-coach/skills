# Clinical Trial Navigation Guide

## ClinicalTrials.gov Search Strategy

### Basic Search
1. Go to clinicaltrials.gov/search
2. **Condition**: Your cancer type + subtype (be specific)
3. **Status**: "Recruiting"
4. **Location**: Your city/state or "United States"

### Advanced Filters
- **Phase**: I, II, III
- **Intervention**: Specific drug or class
- **Funder**: NCI, Industry, Academic

### Understanding Status Codes
| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Recruiting | Actively enrolling — apply now |
| Not yet recruiting | Approved but not open — check back |
| Active, not recruiting | Enrolled patients being treated — too late |
| Completed | Trial finished |
| Suspended/Terminated | Paused or stopped |

## Trial Phases

| Phase | Purpose | Patients | Risk Level |
|-------|---------|---------|-----------|
| Phase I | Safety, dosing | 10-30 | Highest — first-in-human |
| Phase II | Efficacy signal | 30-100 | Moderate |
| Phase III | Comparison to standard | 100-1000+ | Lower — more data available |

## Eligibility Assessment
- **Inclusion**: Diagnosis, biomarkers, stage requirements
- **Exclusion**: Prior treatments, organ function, performance status
- **Performance status (ECOG 0-2)** usually required
- Some criteria are negotiable — always ask

## Trial Evaluation Checklist
- [ ] Cancer type and molecular profile match?
- [ ] Meet eligibility criteria?
- [ ] Comfortable with this phase's risk level?
- [ ] Placebo arm? Odds of active treatment?
- [ ] Travel requirements manageable?
- [ ] Additional testing/visits acceptable?
- [ ] What happens at trial end?
- [ ] Uncovered costs?
