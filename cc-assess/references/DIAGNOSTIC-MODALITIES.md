# Diagnostic Modalities Reference

## Standard Pathology
| Modality | What It Measures | Cost | Turnaround |
|----------|-----------------|------|-----------|
| H&E Staining | Cell morphology, tissue architecture | Included in biopsy | 3-5 days |
| IHC | Specific protein expression (ER, PR, HER2, PD-L1) | $200-$500/marker | 3-7 days |
| FISH | Gene amplifications and translocations | $500-$1,000 | 5-10 days |

## Imaging
| Modality | Best For | Cost | Notes |
|----------|---------|------|-------|
| CT (contrast) | Most solid tumors, staging | $1,000-$3,000 | Workhorse of cancer imaging |
| MRI | Brain, spine, soft tissue, sarcomas | $1,500-$4,000 | Superior soft tissue detail |
| PET-CT | Metabolic activity, staging, response | $3,000-$6,000 | Shows if tumor is metabolically active |
| Bone Scan | Skeletal metastases | $500-$1,500 | Prostate, breast with bone suspicion |
| Ultrasound | Thyroid, liver, guided biopsies | $200-$800 | Quick, no radiation |

## Advanced Sequencing
| Modality | What It Adds | Cost | When to Use |
|----------|-------------|------|-------------|
| WES | All protein-coding genes | $1,000-$3,000 | Rare cancer, no panel targets |
| WGS | Entire genome, structural variants | $1,000-$5,000 | Complex variants, comprehensive |
| Bulk RNA-seq | Gene expression, fusions | $1,000-$3,000 | Therapy selection, fusion detection |
| scRNA-seq | Individual cell expression | $5,000-$15,000 | Immune infiltrate, heterogeneity |
| Spatial transcriptomics | Expression + tissue location | $5,000-$20,000 | Immune-tumor spatial relationships |
| Long-read (Nanopore) | Structural variants, methylation | $2,000-$5,000 | Complex rearrangements |
