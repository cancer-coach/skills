# Diagnostic Options: A Discussion Guide

Sources checked: **2026-10-15**. Educational support; the oncology team selects tests and interprets results.

## Start with the decision

Ask what a proposed test could change, how soon the answer is needed, and what happens if testing is declined or inconclusive. More testing is not automatically better. Do not delay necessary care while researching optional tests.

| Category | Useful question for the team |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Pathology and specialist review | Is the diagnosis/subtype established, and would expert review resolve uncertainty? |
| Imaging | What clinical question does this scan answer, and what are its risks and limitations? |
| Blood or other laboratory testing | Which result needs action, who reviews it, and how will I hear back? |
| Tumor biomarker testing | Would this assay inform an established treatment decision or trial question for this cancer? |
| Inherited-risk testing | Is genetic counseling or a separate germline test appropriate? |
| Research assays, sequencing, or functional models | Is this research or clinical care, and is there evidence that its use changes outcomes? |

Tumor biomarker testing and inherited-risk testing answer different questions. A matching biomarker does not guarantee treatment benefit, and a tumor result may need separate inherited-risk confirmation. See [NCI: Biomarker Testing for Cancer Treatment](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/types/biomarker-testing-cancer-treatment).

Avoid ranking commercial tests by gene count, advertised sensitivity, or price alone. Ask about the exact assay, sample requirements, clinical indication, uncertainty, tissue use, coverage, and out-of-pocket estimate. Research availability and laboratory quality do not by themselves establish clinical usefulness.

## Test follow-through template

```markdown
Clinical question / intended decision:
Test and ordering clinician:
Ordered date / scheduled date:
Sample requirements and preparation confirmed by:
Expected result date supplied by the team:
Who will review it and explain the result:
How to contact the team if the result is overdue:
Next action and owner:
```

An absent portal message is not a confirmed normal result. Follow up through the team's agreed channel. New severe symptoms need clinical assessment; do not wait for an outstanding test or an AI review.
